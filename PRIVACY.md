# Privacy policy

Spaces Sidebar does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- Your Spaces, folders and pinned tabs are stored in the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device.
- The link between a pinned tab and an open tab is kept in session storage and is cleared when the browser closes.
- A file you import (`StorableSidebar.json`, `bookmarks.html` or a backup) is read locally in the browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.
- The extension makes no network requests and contains no analytics or third-party code.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `sidePanel` | Show the sidebar in Chrome's side panel. |
| `tabs` | List the open tabs of the window with their titles and addresses, pin the current tab, switch to or close a tab. |
| `tabGroups` | Optional "Tabs per Space" mode: one tab group per Space. |
| `storage` | Keep your Spaces and pinned tabs on this device. |
| `favicon` | Show site icons next to pinned tabs. |
